Before you begin

Consent is ongoing.Consent must be freely given, informed, specific, and ongoing. Anyone can say no, pause, set boundaries, or change their mind at any time. A signature never gives advance permission for future activity and never replaces checking in in the moment.

This is a personal acknowledgment template, not a legal guarantee or a substitute for applicable law. If anyone feels pressured, unsure, impaired, or unable to freely choose, stop and do not proceed.
